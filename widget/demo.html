A stand-in for a client's website. The only thing that matters here is the <script> tag at the bottom — that is the whole integration.

Questions the demo corpus can answer:

  • What time is check-in, and can I check in early?
  • Are dogs allowed?
  • What is the cancellation policy?
  • How do I get there from Bagdogra airport?
  • Is breakfast included?

And one it should refuse, because the answer is not in the corpus: "can you book me a table for tomorrow?"
